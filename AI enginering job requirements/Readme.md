## 1. AI & Agentic Systems Development

We design, build, and maintain **production-grade AI applications, autonomous agents, and multi-agent workflows** using modern frameworks such as:

- **LangChain**
- **LlamaIndex**
- **AutoGen**
- Native cloud AI studios: **Azure AI Foundry** and **AWS Bedrock**

> We don't just prototype — we ship reliable, scalable agentic systems built for real-world use.

---

## 2. RAG & Vector Search

We build, optimize, and manage **enterprise Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines** and **vector databases**, including:

- **Pinecone**
- **Qdrant**
- **Chroma**
- **Azure AI Search**

Our expertise ensures LLMs are grounded in accurate, domain-specific knowledge — improving relevance, reducing hallucinations, and enabling trustworthy enterprise answers.

## 3. API Integration & Backend Development

We integrate **foundational and custom LLMs** into existing **backend microservices** via **REST APIs and gRPC**, including:

- **OpenAI**
- **Anthropic**
- **Open-source models** like **Llama** and **DeepSeek**

This allows us to embed AI seamlessly into your current architecture rather than bolting on isolated tools.
